Network (Master/Node)

Wires multiple boards together over ESP-NOW so a Master can relay positioning commands from a PC to servos on other boards. Requires a reboot to take effect after changing.

Connect this board to a PC over USB serial at 115200 baud and send one JSON object per line, e.g. {"node":3,"angle":120.5} (node:0 = all nodes), or {"cmd":"list"} to list known nodes. See docs/serial-protocol.md.
